# Refinement-contract decision

## `SUPPORTED_PROOF_SEARCH_CONTRACT`

A refinement contract independently defines a locally derivable, immutable, future-independent rank supporting certified **bounded proof-search** descent. This conclusion is narrow and conditional: it accepts maximum depth when genuinely supplied before search, and exact minimum real width for the registered dyadic real-split rule. It does not claim the allowance is CDC-native, geometrically necessary, sufficient for classification, or independently proved by fractal dynamics.

## Answer to the governing question

The depth-two numeric assignment is not self-justifying. It rests on a genuine contract **only if** allowance 2 was an independently issued, immutable bound on admissible proof search rather than chosen from the desired tree. Under that contract, ranks mean remaining permitted refinements and certify bounded transformations; they do not predict or guarantee proof success. If provenance cannot establish independent issuance, the historical `REGISTERED-LOCAL-REFINEMENT-BUDGET-V1` value remains observationally indistinguishable from preselected depth. This study supplies precise semantics and validation for future/replayed issuance without retroactively changing the old certificate.

The minimum-width contract gives the same ranks for the existing tree from a distinct geometric input, demonstrating that contract-relative ranks can coincide without being interchangeable. Direct-orbit `NEGATIVE_RESULT`, composite-object requirement, single discharge, one-level descent, and `SUPPORTED_DEPTH_TWO_COMPOSITION` remain unchanged.

## Limitations and next study

Only the exact target, binary64 dyadics, real halving, depths/tokens through two, and width floor `1/16` are registered. There is no arbitrary recursion, general fractal completeness/boundedness theorem, scheduler, performance result, arbitrary precision, renderer, memory, Android, JNI, Kotlin, Ouro, or legacy change. Recommended next study: define a provenance-signed contract issuance record and investigate an independently proved conventional guard-distance/enclosure threshold; do not enlarge depth until one of those inputs supplies a non-cosmetic allowance.
