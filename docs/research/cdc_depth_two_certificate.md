Canonical `cdc-depth-two-study-v1` serialization records root R; exact `split_1` and `split_2`; node identities/bounds and lineage; `REGISTERED-LOCAL-REFINEMENT-BUDGET-V1`; creation ranks `2,1,1,0,0`; comparisons `9>6>5`; exact coverage/non-overlap evidence; three independent conventional leaf guards; discharge records; sequential `[9,6,5,4,3,0]` and batch `[9,6,5,0]`; immutable-rank and future-independence assertions; terminal status; direct-orbit `NEGATIVE_RESULT`; `SUPPORTED_ONE_LEVEL_BRANCHING_DESCENT`; and `COMPOSITE_OBJECT_REQUIRED`.

Validation keeps separate: each split's geometry, each child's smaller rank, first descent, second descent with unchanged B, preservation of old records, absence of future-tree input, conventional guard evidence, and complete discharge. False/mismatched fields reject the entire certificate. Determinism comes from fixed field order, exact registered dyadics, and fixed identities; passive notes are not evidence.
