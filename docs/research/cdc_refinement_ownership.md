# Refinement ownership boundary

| Owner | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| conventional mathematics | region validity, exact coverage/non-overlap, guard truth, enclosure/escape theorems, arithmetic assumptions, terminal classification | search allowance, execution order |
| CDC proof transformation | guarded certified obligation replacement/discharge, invariant preservation, proved potential inequality, compatible certificate composition | discovery of fractal facts or initial contract allowance |
| refinement contract | immutable target scope, bounded search capacity, allowed rule/policy version, exhaustion/fallback disposition | theorem truth, scheduler order, wall time |
| scheduler | which eligible obligation/rule runs next; cancellation/queue policy | rank definition, proof validity, terminal theorem |
| user/application | problem request, acceptable resource/fallback policy, issuance of a contract through an accountable owner | retroactive certificate changes |
| memory/runtime | storage, allocation, process limits, representation mechanics | mathematical descent or completeness |

Thus conventional mathematics owns guards and terminal theorems; a contract owner supplies bounded search resources; CDC owns certified transformations under the contract; and the scheduler chooses order without defining validity. Maximum nodes, time, or memory may stop execution but cannot masquerade as descent.
