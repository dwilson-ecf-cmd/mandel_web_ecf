# Two-child split rule

Rule `CDC-TWO-CHILD-SPLIT-V1` is deliberately closed over the exact regions in the region study.

* **Precondition:** the sole active obligation is `ESCAPES(R)`; exact identities, formula, radius, and proof-sheet version match.
* **Geometric predicate:** real split `17/8` produces the two valid, nondegenerate boxes.
* **Conventional proof:** endpoint equality plus the half-open boundary convention proves exact union and disjoint claim ownership; closed evidence enclosures have disjoint interiors.
* **Strict-size proof:** area units relative to `q=1/32` are `2 -> 1,1`.
* **CDC certification:** replace the parent obligation only after the geometry proof and the registered multiset/token comparison `3 -> 2` both validate.
* **Evidence produced:** child identities, coverage, boundary convention, area units, base ranks, and rule identity. Escape evidence is not produced by splitting.
* **Token update:** aggregate potential drops by one; the resulting obligations are the two child escape claims.

Failure of identity, validity, coverage, strict size, or potential comparison rejects the split and retains `S0`. Correct geometry alone is insufficient. This is neither a general partition rule nor a claim that every smaller box supplies CDC progress.
